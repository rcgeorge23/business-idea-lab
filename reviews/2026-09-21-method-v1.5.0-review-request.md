- **Method:** 1.4.0 -> 1.5.0
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Trigger:** Trigger 2 - material change to the discovery method
- **Requested from:** ChatGPT (independent reviewer, routed by the human owner)
- **Expected response file:** `reviews/<date>-method-v1.5.0-chatgpt.md`

## What changed and why

One change: discovery now runs an **opportunity-observation funnel** before full
candidate investigation (`method/discovery.md`, applied by `method/run-protocol.md`
step 3).

> 15-20 raw opportunity observations -> shallow triage -> at most 3 full candidates ->
> the existing rigorous evaluation.

Motivation is empirical. The 1.4.0 source-class budget worked (run
`20260921T085149Z-normal` produced only non-regulatory candidates), but all three
candidates again died on `defensible_wedge` (apispend 40.0, ewsregister 44.6,
agent-checkout-offplatform 49.2). The convergence review concluded the next
experiment should increase **breadth before depth**, not weaken filters. Several
source classes relevant to small bootstrapped opportunities also remained
underexplored: poor/expensive narrow incumbent software, manual re-keying workflows
and awkward integrations.

The funnel:

- Sweeps 15-20 materially distinct **observations** (cheap: no scorecard, dossier or
  evidence level) into `observations/<run-id>.md` using `templates/observation/pool.md`.
- Supports both **change-driven** opportunities (evidenced why-now) and **persistent
  market failures** (answer "why does this problem still persist despite existing
  alternatives?"; never invent a discontinuity).
- Biases the sweep toward poor/expensive narrow incumbent software, manual
  structured-data/spreadsheet/email/PDF workflows and awkward integrations, seeking
  practitioner/community evidence.
- **Shallow-triages** every observation, recording negative evidence and reasons.
- Promotes **at most 3** candidates, with zero/one/two valid and an explicit
  no-filler rule; promoted candidates then go through the existing process unchanged
  and inherit no positive evidence from triage.

The 1.4.0 source-class budget is retained unchanged and applies at promotion (no
second quota). Limits raised for funnel runs: web lookups 25 -> 40, agent steps
80 -> 120.

Unchanged: scorecard weights, threshold 65, hard-filter semantics
(`pass`/`unknown`/`fail`), `defensible_wedge`, evidence-level definitions, lifecycle
gates, candidate maximum 3, advancement maximum 1, falsification-first behaviour.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Does a fixed pool target of 15-20 risk quantity over quality - i.e. padding,
   near-duplicates or trivia - and is the no-filler plus materially-distinct rule
   enough to keep the pool honest?
2. Does shallow triage risk killing observations that deserve candidate treatment
   (false negatives), and should triage rejections ever be sampled for review the
   way killed ideas are?
3. Persistent-market-failure candidates keep `why_now.strength: absent`, so the
   v1.2.0 cap on `differentiation` and `problem_severity_frequency` still applies at
   Plausible/Promising. Is that the right treatment, or does it systematically
   penalise exactly the underexplored class the funnel is meant to find?
4. Is one post-change run enough to judge the funnel, and what evidence would
   justify retaining, resizing (e.g. more/fewer observations) or reverting it?
