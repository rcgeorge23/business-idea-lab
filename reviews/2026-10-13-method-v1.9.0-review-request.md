- **Method:** 1.8.2 -> 1.9.0

## Where this comes from

Issue #21 (owner, opened 2026-10-10) pivots the laboratory to **self-service
micro-SaaS** discovery under a hard acquisition constraint: no direct sales,
networking, interviews, demos, outbound, procurement or partnerships; organic search
as the primary channel; self-service signup/payment; no mandatory demo or
hand-holding; buildable and supportable by one solo developer part-time. The prior
method was regulatory-change-first. Its recent runs were converging on zero
candidates, and the owner asked for a funnel that starts from query/task intent and
judges candidates on an evidenced organic-search route and self-service fit. Several
retrospectives (`retrospectives/`) had already recommended a human-led
sourcing/framing review; this change is the worker-side response under issue #21.

The change is material: it alters the discovery funnel, the scorecard weights and
dimensions, the required evidence artefacts (a new `seo_assessment` object), the hard
filters, and the lifecycle caps for a defined archetype. It therefore requires review
under trigger 2 before its outputs can be treated as method-conformant.

## What changed and why

1. **Search-intent-first discovery funnel** (`method/discovery.md`,
   `method/run-protocol.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `scripts/prompts/lab-run.md`). The chain is
   now query -> job/task -> existing solutions -> neglected sub-niche -> paid
   self-service hypothesis. Each run records a shortlist of **3-5 qualified
   hypotheses** (fewer when the evidence is weak; an honest zero is valid). The
   previous 15-20 observation quota is retired; observations remain the raw material,
   not a target. The regulatory/change-driven route is demoted to one route among
   several rather than the default starting point.

2. **SEO evidence standard** (`method/discovery.md`, per-candidate
   `seo_assessment`). Each shortlisted hypothesis records exact seed queries and
   intent clusters, dated demand evidence, the live SERP landscape (named pages,
   dates, ads/forums/gov/AI answers), a qualitative organic-entry assessment,
   self-service conversion mechanics, assumption-labelled traffic-to-revenue
   scenarios, SEO risks (AI overviews/zero-click, seasonality, platform dependency),
   and honest unknowns with low-cost next checks. No volumes, rankings, CPC, trends
   or difficulty may be invented; a blocked check is recorded as unknown, not as
   absence; search volume alone is never evidence of willingness to pay.

3. **Scorecard schema v2** (`method/scorecard.md`, `scripts/validate_repo.py`,
   weights version 1.3.0). Twelve dimensions summing 100: `problem_severity_frequency`
   12, `buyer_budget_clarity` 12, `evidence_strength` 12 (the three gating dimensions,
   each still required >= 3), `seo_discoverability` 14 (new, prominent),
   `self_service_fit` 10 (new, prominent), `founder_fit` 10 (increased; absorbs solo
   build complexity, support burden, maintenance and owner time), `economics` 8
   (including pricing versus traffic economics), `validation_speed_cost` 7,
   `distribution` 5, `feasibility` 3, `differentiation` 3 (downweighted at idea
   stage), `risk` 4. Threshold remains 65; at least **9 of 12** dimensions must be
   scored for `validation-ready`.

4. **Three new hard filters** on top of the nine general ones: `seo_discoverable`,
   `self_serve_purchase`, `solo_operator_fit`. Semantics are unchanged: `fail` kills,
   `pass` requires cited evidence, `unknown` requires a `resolve_via`.

5. **Search-led archetype D** (`why_now.strength: not-required`). An evergreen
   search-led niche needs no contrived discontinuity; its demand case lives in the
   `seo_assessment`. The archetype-aware missing-why-now cap (v1.6.0) simply does not
   apply to archetype D. This is an exemption from a cap only: it is not a bonus, it
   lifts no other cap, and it upgrades no hard filter.

6. **Legacy preservation and version-aware validation.** Schema v1 scorecards are
   frozen exactly as recorded (weights 1.2.0, method <= 1.8.2). The validator accepts
   both versions with the correct per-version tables; new scorecards must be schema 2
   and may not be written for a legacy method. Legacy ideas are re-triaged opt-in via
   the new additive procedure `method/retriage.md`, which never rewrites a historic
   score, decision or review outcome.

7. **Machinery and tests.** `scripts/validate_repo.py` is version-aware;
   `scripts/build_dashboard.py` and `scripts/build_review_request.py` render both
   versions (including the SEO assessment); `scripts/finalize_run.py` accepts a
   `representative` run mode; `tests/` now contains a stdlib unittest suite (35 tests
   at the time of writing). The latent-signal pass (v1.8.2), triage false-negative
   audit and source-class budget are retained unchanged.

## Counterarguments and failure conditions

- Without paid rank or volume tooling, the SEO demand evidence available to this
  worker may often be thin. The method forbids inventing it, so many candidates may
  stall at `unknown` filters instead of being researched to a conclusion. If that
  happens repeatedly, the funnel is too demanding for the available tools.
- AI overviews and zero-click results may degrade organic entry feasibility faster
  than the method assumes; the method records the risk but cannot resolve it.
- Downweighting `differentiation` to 3 may select commodity ideas; the new
  `seo_discoverability` and `self_service_fit` dimensions are intended to compensate,
  but the trade may be wrong.
- The archetype-D exemption could be misused to skip demand evidence. The mitigation
  is that the exemption removes only the missing-why-now cap and rests on a required,
  inspectable `seo_assessment`.
- `solo_operator_fit` is judgement-heavy and could be applied inconsistently.
- The demonstration used one representative run executed by the same worker that
  wrote the method; it is not independent evidence that the method works.
- The legacy compatibility branch is intentionally permissive for historic cards;
  this keeps the ledger auditable but could mask accidental drift if a new card were
  ever mislabelled as legacy.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Are the v2 weights defensible relative to the owner's acquisition constraint,
   especially `seo_discoverability` 14, `self_service_fit` 10, `founder_fit` 10 and
   `differentiation` 3?
2. Is the required `seo_assessment` achievable honestly with the tools a worker of
   this kind can use, or should some fields be explicitly optional?
3. Is the archetype-D `not-required` exemption safe, or does it open a
   demand-evidence loophole?
4. Does the version-aware validator preserve auditability without inviting
   mislabelling?
5. Is one representative run a sufficient demonstration, or should the owner run a
   dry-run under the new prompt before accepting the method?
6. What would make this pivot wrong, and what cheap checks would reveal it?
