## Overall assessment

Method 1.6.0 adequately addresses the changes requested in the v1.5.0 review without weakening the lab's rejection standard. The observation funnel should be retained and the method is ready for another normal discovery run.

No further method change is required before that run.

## 1. Persistence thesis

The two-part test is sufficiently tight for the next empirical run.

A persistent-market-failure candidate can avoid the missing-`why_now` cap only when affirmative evidence supports both:

1. continued buyer pain/cost/workaround despite alternatives that are actually reachable by the target segment; and
2. a credible present-tense mechanism explaining why that market failure persists.

This is materially stronger than accepting generic "evergreen pain." Unsupported claims such as "incumbents do not care" or "the niche is too small" remain insufficient. The rule does not confer a scoring bonus, change a hard filter, or establish a wedge.

The regressions are reassuring: Aucly pre-launch was not artificially rescued, while ShiftSwap, WonkyBox and GrantScout remain rejected for their independent hard-filter/veto failures.

## 2. Sampled triage audit

Exactly one sampled audit per normal funnel run is an appropriate starting cadence.

The purpose is calibration, not statistical estimation. One deliberately selected high-ambiguity rejection gives useful information about whether shallow triage is confusing competitor existence with adequate occupation without materially eroding the cost advantage of the funnel.

Do not increase the cadence yet. Revisit only if audits are repeatedly overturned, pool size changes materially, or evidence emerges that a particular triage rejection class is systematically unreliable.

## 3. Competitor-existence clarification

The clarification is appropriate and does not itself weaken `defensible_wedge`.

The O14 audit is a good demonstration of the intended distinction. The underlying workflow pain appears substantial, but additional research found multiple vendors with demonstrated deployments occupying the relevant automation seam. The rejection was therefore upheld because of evidence of adequate occupation, not merely because named competitors exist.

Future runs should continue to distinguish:
- category competition;
- adjacent features;
- claimed capability;
- demonstrated occupation of the exact seam for the defined buyer.

A crowded category can still contain an underserved niche, but the burden remains on the candidate to provide affirmative evidence for that niche rather than infer it from dissatisfaction alone.

## 4. Regression evidence

The supplied regressions are sufficient for approving this method revision:

- **Aucly pre-launch:** unchanged at the historical 49.5 / parked; no discontinuity manufactured and no unsupported persistence thesis used to lift the cap.
- **ShiftSwap:** remains killed.
- **WonkyBox:** remains killed.
- **GrantScout:** remains killed.
- **O14 insurance broker submission re-keying:** triage rejection upheld after the new sampled audit.

The regression set does not prove the new rule is optimal, but it adequately shows that the requested correction has not obviously revived the known weak/crowded fixtures.

## 5. Pool size and funnel

Retain the current **15–20 observations → shallow triage → ≤3 full candidates** structure for the next several runs.

Do not increase observation volume yet. The first funnel run nearly consumed its lookup budget, and we do not yet know whether additional breadth would improve the quality of promoted candidates.

The next useful evidence should come from running the calibrated method rather than further editing it.

## 6. What to monitor in the next run

The next normal run should proceed under Method 1.6.0 with no additional scoring/filter changes. Its retrospective should pay particular attention to:

- whether persistent-market-failure observations can satisfy the new two-part persistence test with real evidence rather than narrative;
- whether the sampled triage audit upholds or overturns its selected rejection;
- whether the funnel again promotes only incumbent-captured seams;
- whether the same `defensible_wedge` cluster persists despite broader sourcing;
- whether the observation pool shows padding/duplication as it approaches 20;
- lookup/step budget pressure;
- whether any promoted candidate is genuinely stronger than the candidates from prior runs.

A run that advances nothing remains a valid outcome.

## 7. No new implementation ticket required before rerun

There are no changes requested by this review.

After this response is reconciled into review state, Method 1.6.0 can be used for the next normal discovery cycle. A separate implementation ticket is unnecessary unless the project's process requires a ticket simply to authorise/rerun a normal cycle.

Do not combine approval with changes to scoring, threshold, `defensible_wedge`, evidence levels, lifecycle gates, pool size, or audit cadence.

## Condition that would change the verdict

Revisit Method 1.6.0 if subsequent runs show one or more of the following:

- persistent candidates routinely escape the cap on weak narrative evidence;
- sampled triage audits repeatedly overturn rejections;
- the competitor-occupation clarification results in obvious crowded propositions being promoted;
- the 15–20 pool produces substantial padding or inadequate evidence due to lookup limits; or
- repeated runs demonstrate that the funnel still explores essentially the same opportunity shapes despite nominal source diversity.

Until then, further methodological tuning would risk overfitting the lab to its calibration fixtures rather than learning from new runs.
