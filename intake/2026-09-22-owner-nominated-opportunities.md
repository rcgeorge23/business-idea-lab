## I1 — Business Idea Lab as a validation service (non-regulatory)

- **Buyer/problem hypothesis:** A founder considering a costly build may want a candid, auditable assessment and a cheap experiment they can actually run, rather than an encouraging AI score. Which founders have budget and will pay *before* building is unknown.
- **Potential operational seam:** A fixed-scope human-reviewed desk assessment that identifies disconfirming evidence, followed by optional assistance executing a buyer-facing test. Distinguish what desk research can establish from actual customer demand.
- **Existing alternatives:** [WhyHire](https://whyhire.ai/startups/idea-validation) sells an evidence-backed idea-research service; [EVIDR](https://www.evidr-ai.com/) offers customer-interview-led validation; [Finsight](https://www.gofinsight.com/idea-validation-sprint.html) offers a financial feasibility sprint; [StartupValidation](https://startupvalidation.com/) sells rapid AI reports. These first-party pages establish competitors and positioning, **not** sales volume or our wedge (checked 2026-09-22).
- **Critical unknowns:** Reachable buyer, willingness to pay, trust/credibility, time to deliver, repeatability, and whether a paid service can honestly claim more value than a founder using AI plus direct interviews.
- **Cheap disconfirming test to propose, not execute without approval:** Offer a tightly specified paid pilot to a small number of unrelated target buyers; measure commitments and delivery hours. No outreach is authorised by this intake.
- **Possible archetype:** Persistent market failure, but its two-part persistence thesis is not yet evidenced. Do not lift the missing-why-now cap by assertion.

## I2 — Making Tax Digital transition for a narrow landlord/sole-trader cohort (regulatory)

- **Legal change:** HMRC says the qualifying-income threshold is £30,000 from 6 April 2027 and £20,000 from 6 April 2028; [official guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/get-ready-for-mtd-an-agent-toolkit/understanding-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax) and [population estimates](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-business-population-statistics/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-business-population-statistics-commentary) (checked 2026-09-22).
- **Buyer/job hypothesis:** A specific group with fragmented records may need help cleaning historical data, choosing compatible software, and collaborating with an accountant before digital reporting starts.
- **Second-order seam to investigate:** Transition/data cleanup and reconciliation for one niche, not another generic MTD filing product.
- **Incumbent/free check:** HMRC already directs users to [compatible and bridging software](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/choose-the-right-software-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax). Test whether the specific handoff remains painful despite those offerings.
- **Critical unknowns:** Which niche has measurable manual work, who pays (taxpayer or practice), time/cost per onboarding, and whether accountants already bundle it.

## I3 — Guaranteed-hours and shift-notice workflow (regulatory)

- **Legal status:** The [Employment Rights Act implementation timeline](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/implementing-the-plan-to-make-work-pay-and-employment-rights-act/plan-to-make-work-pay-and-employment-rights-act-timeline-update) places guaranteed hours, reasonable notice and short-notice payments in 2027, with timing to be updated after consultation; future dates remain subject to parliamentary processes (checked 2026-09-22).
- **Buyer/job hypothesis:** A small multi-site employer using spreadsheets or a basic rota tool may struggle to reconcile scheduled hours, actual hours, notice changes and exceptions.
- **Second-order seam to investigate:** Evidence and exception reconciliation between rota, payroll and contracts, not generic HR-policy content.
- **Critical unknowns:** Final rules; affected worker patterns; what current rota/payroll products already provide; adviser channel; buyer budget.
- **Deduplication:** Compare with existing `reasonable-steps` (workplace harassment-prevention evidence): same broad employment-law source class, but a different job and buyer workflow. Do not treat distinct wording alone as proof of a distinct commercial seam.

## I4 — Contractual control agreements reporting handoff (regulatory)

- **Legal change:** [HM Land Registry guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/contractual-control-agreements) says certain agreements affecting registered land in England and Wales must be submitted digitally through a regulated conveyancer from 6 April 2027 (checked 2026-09-22).
- **Buyer/job hypothesis:** Development/property teams and conveyancers may need to gather agreement terms, parties, dates, variations and evidence for filing while avoiding duplicate entry from deal-management systems.
- **Second-order seam to investigate:** A narrow document-to-filing handoff or exception queue, not a generic legal deadline reminder.
- **Critical unknowns:** Volume per firm, submission interface/API, existing conveyancing case-management support, willingness to pay, legal-professional liability, and whether government tooling is already adequate.
- **Gate:** Do not promote until buyer-side evidence shows the handoff is frequent and underserved.

## Intake handling

For each item, record `verified / changed / disproved` source status; the legal state (`consultation`, `Bill`, `enacted-not-commenced`, `in force`); jurisdiction; affected buyer; mandatory task; existing public/free solution; named incumbents; evidence of current workaround or spend; distribution route; and a cheap falsifying test.
