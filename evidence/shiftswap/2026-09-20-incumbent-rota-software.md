# Evidence: incumbent UK rota/shift software already bundles shift swaps

- **Idea / scope:** `shiftswap`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** vendor websites: rotaapp.uk, rotadone.co.uk, rotahubapp.co.uk, rotakeep.co.uk, clockrota.com, wavroapp.com, batchstaff.com, staffhq.app, shiftbase.com
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-20
- **Credibility:** vendor claim — self-published pricing and feature marketing. Weak secondary; used only to establish that comparable features exist and at what advertised price, not to validate demand.

## Claim(s) supported

- Multiple UK rota products already include self-service shift swaps at low or zero cost.
- Advertised entry prices are £0–£49/month, with several free or near-free tiers.
- At least one product advertises "shift swaps" as a standard Starter-tier feature at £49/mo per location (up to 20 staff).

## Exact detail

- RotaApp (rotaapp.uk): "Trusted by 40+ UK shift teams"; staff swap self-serve.
- RotaHub (rotahubapp.co.uk): Starter £49/mo per location (up to 20 staff), includes "Time-off requests & shift swaps".
- ClockRota (clockrota.com): £0 public beta; £9/mo tier up to 5 staff; includes shift swaps.
- BatchStaff (batchstaff.com): £10/month unlimited team, swaps in-app.
- RotaKeep (rotakeep.co.uk): shift swaps, no per-user fees.
- Others advertising swaps or equivalent: Rota Done (rotadone.co.uk), Wavro (wavroapp.com), StaffHQ (staffhq.app — "500+ UK businesses", shift-swap requests live), Shiftbase (shiftbase.com, marketed as "Best rota software for hospitality UK 2026").

## Why it is credible

These are live vendor pages, sufficient to establish feature parity and advertised price points. They are marketing material, so adoption and actual usage cannot be inferred.

## What it does NOT show

- Does not prove these products are good, widely adopted, or that venues are satisfied — only that shift-swap functionality is a standard, low-priced commodity.
- No independent data on churn, satisfaction or real payback.
