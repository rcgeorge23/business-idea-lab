# Evidence: free authoritative UK government grant discovery

- **Idea / scope:** `grantscout`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** GOV.UK "Find government grants" guidance (gov.uk/guidance/find-government-grants); "Find a grant" service (find-government-grants.service.gov.uk); business.gov.uk funding options page
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-20
- **Credibility:** primary — official UK government services.

## Claim(s) supported

- The UK government already provides a free, searchable/filterable grant discovery service covering voluntary/community/social-enterprise organisations, individuals, SMEs and other businesses.
- The service is explicitly expanding its coverage ("More grants will be added").
- Government guidance says grants involve long application processes and competition.

## Exact detail

- "Find a grant" (find-government-grants.service.gov.uk) is free and offers search/filter across government grants; a filtered query returned, for example, 84 results at the time of access.
- GOV.UK guidance page "Find government grants" directs users to the free service and frames grants as competitive with long application processes.
- business.gov.uk's funding-options page links to the same free GOV.UK grant checks.

## Why it is credible

Official government web properties are the authoritative source for what government funding exists and how to find it.

## What it does NOT show

- It does not show that discovery is the customer's real bottleneck — the pain may be application quality/success rate rather than matching.
- It does not show SMEs are unaware of or dissatisfied with the free service; that is an assumption.
