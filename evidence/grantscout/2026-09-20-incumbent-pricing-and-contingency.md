# Evidence: incumbent grant-matching pricing and contingency-fee alternatives

- **Idea / scope:** `grantscout`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** GovOwed pricing page (govowed.uk/pricing); grant consultancies: ukgrantfunding.co.uk / Tatton Consulting, granttree.co.uk, grantscentre.co.uk, grantup.co.uk
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-20
- **Credibility:** vendor claim — self-published pricing and success-rate marketing. Weak secondary only; vendor claims never establish demand.

## Claim(s) supported

- A direct micro-competitor, GovOwed, already sells grant matching at a £0 free scan and £29 one-time per company report, £199 for a 10-report pack, and £499/year unlimited reports for accountants/advisers.
- GovOwed's report includes drafted applications for matches and 12 months of re-runs.
- Grant consultancies price on no-win/no-fee or success-fee models, anchoring the category to contingency pricing.

## Exact detail

- GovOwed (govowed.uk/pricing, captured 2026-09-20): "Free scan" £0; "Company report" £29 one-time per company, includes "Drafted applications for your matches" + 12 months re-runs; "10-report pack" £199; "Practice" £499/year unlimited, targeting accountants/advisers. Pitch text: "A 20% success fee on a £50,000 grant is £10,000. This is £29." and "Success-fee agencies: them 10-30% of your award, or £2,750+ up front".
- Tatton Consulting / ukgrantfunding.co.uk: no-win-no-fee guarantee; from £3,500 + 7–12% success fee, or success-only 10–15% approval fee; claims 5–15% DIY success rate vs ~75% with support.
- GrantTree (granttree.co.uk): claims 65% of worked applications won vs a 3–7% competition average.
- The Grants Centre (grantscentre.co.uk): "No Grant, No Fee", success fee 10% up to £75k.
- GrantUp (grantup.co.uk): a further contingency-fee consultancy.

## Why it is credible

These are live vendor pages establishing that the proposed wedge is already occupied and at what advertised prices. They are marketing claims, so success rates and adoption are unverified.

## What it does NOT show

- Does not prove GovOwed is successful or that customers are satisfied — only that the exact position (flat-fee grant matching for UK SMEs) is already taken while the authoritative alternative is free.
- Success-rate comparisons are self-reported.
