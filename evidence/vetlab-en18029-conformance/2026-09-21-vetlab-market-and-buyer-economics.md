# Evidence: veterinary laboratory software market size and a comparable public tender

- **Idea / scope:** `vetlab-en18029-conformance`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** MarketsandMarkets veterinary software market report (marketsandmarkets.com), 2025 data with 2030 forecast; bidstats.uk tender award PURCH2873 (veterinary laboratory LIMS); esremedia 2026-07-30
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21
- **Credibility:** credible secondary

## Claim(s) supported

- The UK veterinary software market is estimated at $78.5M in 2025, growing to $102.3M by 2030 — a modest single-digit-percent growth rate.
- Within that, the "veterinary diagnostic laboratories" software segment is estimated at only $6.35M in 2025, growing to $8.07M by 2030.
- A public tender for a veterinary laboratory LIMS (bidstats PURCH2873) was valued at £202,652 and attracted a single offer.

## Exact detail

- MarketsandMarkets: UK veterinary software $78.5M (2025) → $102.3M (2030); veterinary diagnostic laboratories software segment $6.35M (2025) → $8.07M (2030).
- bidstats PURCH2873: veterinary LIMS requirement valued at £202,652 with 1 offer received.
- The adjacent interoperability/standards slice of that segment is not separately sized by any source found.

## Why it is credible

Analyst market sizing and a published public-tender award are both checkable secondary sources. They are used here to bound the commercial scale of the buyer population, not to establish demand.

## What it does NOT show

It does not show the size, budget or willingness to pay of a conformance-tooling buyer; the "diagnostic laboratories software" segment covers full LIMS rather than conformance or mapping tooling, and no source sizes the interoperability/standards sub-slice. The tender shows a small procurement with a single bidder, which is consistent with a thin, specialist buyer population but does not prove it. No lab or vendor budget for conformance work is evidenced.
