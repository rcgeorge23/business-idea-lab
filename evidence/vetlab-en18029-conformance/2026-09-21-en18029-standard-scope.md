# Evidence: DIN EN 18029 standard scope and its deliberate exclusion of code lists

- **Idea / scope:** `vetlab-en18029-conformance`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** DIN catalogue record for DIN EN 18029:2026-04 (E) (din.de); Austrian Standards record for ÖNORM EN 18029:2026-04-15 (austrian-standards.at)
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21
- **Credibility:** primary

## Claim(s) supported

- DIN EN 18029:2026-04 (E), "Animal health diagnostic analyses – Electronic data exchange in laboratory analysis", was published in April 2026 (German version EN 18029:2025).
- ÖNORM EN 18029:2026-04-15 is the Austrian publication of the same European standard.
- The standard specifies a common format for electronic exchange between veterinary prescribers and laboratories, covering request, result and acknowledgement files plus a data dictionary.
- The standard deliberately excludes the code lists (reference lists/nomenclatures) that would be required for unambiguous exchange; adopters must supply or agree those lists themselves.

## Exact detail

- Title and date: DIN EN 18029:2026-04 (E), publication April 2026; the German-language edition is dated 2026-04 and corresponds to EN 18029:2025. ÖNORM EN 18029 is dated 2026-04-15.
- Content: the standard defines the electronic message exchange for laboratory analysis in animal health — request (order), result and acknowledgement (status/confirmation) files — together with a data dictionary describing the fields.
- Code lists: the scope and structure omit the controlled vocabularies/reference lists needed to interpret the coded values unambiguously, so each adopting pair must maintain or agree its own mappings.

## Why it is credible

Primary standards-catalogue records from the national standards bodies; the standard number, edition and date are directly verifiable from those records.

## What it does NOT show

The catalogue records establish publication and scope only. They do not show that any PIMS, LIMS, laboratory or insurer has implemented EN 18029, that any regulator will mandate it, or that any buyer has a funded conformance requirement. They also do not quantify the market for conformance tooling.
