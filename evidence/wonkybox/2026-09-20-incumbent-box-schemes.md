# Evidence: incumbent UK surplus-produce box schemes and advertised prices

- **Idea / scope:** `wonkybox`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** Oddbox (oddbox.co.uk) pricing as reported by The Independent, 2025-02-06; Wonky Veg Boxes (wonkyvegboxes.co.uk) about/pricing page, 2025-11-03
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-20
- **Credibility:** mixed — The Independent is credible secondary for Oddbox pricing; Wonky Veg Boxes pricing is a vendor claim.

## Claim(s) supported

- Oddbox is an established London-based box scheme (founded 2016, Series B, ~85 staff) with published consumer pricing.
- A second UK operator, Wonky Veg Boxes (Leicester), already runs weekly/fortnightly surplus-produce subscriptions from ~£10.49.
- Consumer box prices sit in the £10–£18 range plus delivery, i.e. a low-ticket, price-sensitive category.

## Exact detail

- Oddbox (via The Independent, 2025-02-06): large veg box £17.99; small fruit & veg box £14.95 + £1.99 delivery.
- Wonky Veg Boxes (wonkyvegboxes.co.uk, about-us page 2025-11-03): Leicester-based, weekly/fortnightly wonky veg subscription, boxes from £10.49.
- Oddbox background: founded 2016, London, Series B, ~85 staff (company materials/press).

## Why it is credible

Pricing from a national outlet and the operators' own pages establishes the price band and the existence of multiple incumbents. It does not establish demand quality.

## What it does NOT show

- No independent data on subscriber numbers, retention or regional saturation.
- Does not show whether a differentiated wedge (e.g. B2B, local-only, no-delivery pickup) could work — only that the direct DTC consumer-box position is occupied and cheap.
