## Claim(s) supported

1. From **6 April 2027**, income tax and Class 1A NIC on the most common benefits in kind — company cars, car fuel, vans, van fuel, and employer-provided medical benefits — must be calculated in real time and reported through RTI via payroll software; most remaining benefits follow from **6 April 2028**; loans and accommodation remain outside mandation.
2. The enabling secondary legislation is in force-in-waiting: **SI 2026/189**, made 2026-09-14, removes the employer option to voluntarily register for payrolling from tax year 2027-28 and adjusts P11D submission for ceasing businesses.
3. HMRC has published interim guidance and draft legislation (2025-11-26) covering the operational detail (real-time reporting, the 50% tax-collection limit, P800/Simple Assessment handling, reporting Class 1A NIC in real time, leavers, M&A, moving employees between payrolls).
4. The reporting-data burden is being standardised: per ATT, RTI fields are reduced **from 126 to 32**, of which 14 already exist for cars and **software developers must build 18**.
5. Advisory firms (ADP, Saffery) flag that affected employers must review **how benefit information is collected, validated and transferred into payroll** during the year, and note the cash-flow effect of Class 1A NIC moving from an annual July payment to a per-pay-period accrual.

## Exact detail

- Operative date: 6 April 2027 (phase 1) and 6 April 2028 (phase 2). The measure was originally planned for April 2026 and was deferred on 28 April 2025 to give employers, payroll professionals, software providers and agents more time.
- The government expects **3.5 million people** to have income tax on BiKs collected in real time rather than in arrears.
- No registration is required for the mandated benefits; HMRC will automatically remove those benefits from employees' tax codes. Voluntary registration for loans/accommodation is expected to open November 2026 with a 5 April 2027 deadline.
- Basic PAYE Tools (HMRC's own software) will be updated to handle payrolling from April 2027.
- Key field/technical specifications for developers are expected to be updated during 2026 for the April 2027 regime.

## Why it is credible

Primary government policy paper and statutory instrument, corroborated by professional bodies (ATT, CIOT, ICAEW) who negotiated the phasing, and by a major payroll vendor (Sage) and payroll-service provider (ADP). Multiple independent, dated sources agree on scope, dates and phase split.

## What it does NOT show

- **It is not evidence of buyer pain or budget.** No employer, payroll bureau or accountant is cited as willing to pay for a new data-collection/reconciliation product, and no comparable existing spend is documented.
- **It does not show the seam is unserved.** Nothing cited establishes whether leasing/fleet providers, medical insurers and fuel-card providers already supply machine-readable benefit data to employer payroll, or whether payroll vendors already ingest it.
- Advisory recommendations to "review data collection" are vendor/professional commentary, not observed demand.
- It does not show a durable, defensible wedge versus payroll software vendors and benefits platforms that are already building the adjacent RTI fields.
