# Evidence: DIN EN 18029:2026-04 veterinary lab data exchange standard

- **Idea / scope:** `vetlab-bridge`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** din.de, DIN EN 18029:2026-04 (E), "Animal health diagnostic analyses – Electronic data exchange in laboratory analysis"
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21 (standard dated 2026-04)
- **Credibility:** primary (published standard record)

## Claim(s) supported

- A European standard for electronic exchange of veterinary laboratory analysis data was published in April 2026, specifying request, result and acknowledgement files, a data dictionary and reference lists.
- This is a dated, recent change that could reduce the mapping cost of a semantic-layer bridge and signal regulatory/professional momentum toward standardised veterinary lab exchange.

## Exact detail

- Title and designation verified on the DIN catalogue record: DIN EN 18029:2026-04 (E).
- It is a standard, not a mandate: no evidence was found that it is legally required in any jurisdiction.

## Why it is credible

DIN is the German national standards body and the record is a primary catalogue entry for a published European standard.

## What it does NOT show

- It does NOT establish that any PIMS or lab has implemented it, nor that implementation is required.
- It does not show UK adoption or that it applies to companion-animal specialist labs.
- It does not create a buyer budget by itself; it only changes the technical feasibility/standard-framing of a bridge.
