# Evidence: incumbent bridges and lab↔PIMS integrations

- **Idea / scope:** `vetlab-bridge`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** datahubvet.com; manu-inc.com/integrations (ManuCare); IDEXX integrations pages; pupilot.co/integrations/robovet; software.covetrus.com LabLink
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21
- **Credibility:** vendor claim (vendor marketing and product pages)

## Claim(s) supported

- A live competitor already markets a unified, normalised veterinary data API into long-tail PIMS: DataHub Vet connects to AVImark, Cornerstone, Impromed, eVetPractice, Shepherd and NaVetor via a local in-clinic agent, exposing "structured, normalized veterinary data".
- ManuCare markets a universal lab-import sidecar: CSV/HL7 import of any lab's results into the patient case, "zero copy-paste", explicitly positioned against manual copy-paste.
- IDEXX lists many independent/reference labs already integrated with ezyVet, Cornerstone and Neo (Awanui, B&L, Bionote, Gribbles, MicroVet, Midwest Vet Lab, MiLab, National Bio Vet, SVS, Zoetis).
- Covetrus LabLink auto-imports IDEXX Reference Labs results into RoboVet and transfers in-house analyser results; PupPilot offers a lightweight local connector into on-prem RoboVet and claims 130+ PIMS supported.

## Exact detail

- The connection/structural layer is therefore contested by at least three live products (DataHub Vet, ManuCare, PupPilot/Covetrus LabLink) plus the middleware named in other entries (Bitwerx, Covetrus Connect, AllyDVM).
- The lab-side integrations are concentrated on the cloud PIMS (ezyVet, Cornerstone, Neo) owned by aggregators, consistent with the "long tail left behind" finding elsewhere.

## Why it is credible

These are first-party product pages with named PIMS targets and named labs; they were checked specifically to avoid claiming an unserved seam that is in fact served.

## What it does NOT show

- Vendor pages are marketing: they do not prove adoption, reliability, or coverage of UK specialist/regional labs.
- They do not show whether any of them performs semantic normalisation of results (versus structured transport/attachment), which is the specific gap claimed.
- They do not establish pricing or whether a third entrant can win.
