## Inference (conclusions citing the above)

- I1 (from E1–E5): The mandate is certain and dated. A compulsory, recurring compliance job now exists for every UK practice, with the Order due by 23 Sep 2026 and duties phasing Dec 2026 – Sep 2027. (confidence: high)
- I2 (from E6–E9, E10): The "publish a CMA price list" job is already served by at least four independent providers, two of them free-forever, plus established PMS/website vendors bundling it. The wedge is not defensible. (confidence: high)
- I3 (from E11): The independent segment is real (≈2,382–2,693 practices) but is precisely the segment the free tools court. (confidence: medium)

## Assumptions (falsifiable, not evidence)

- A1: Some independent practices will pay for a compliance tool rather than use a free-forever one. Falsified by a landing-page test showing no conversion at any price point.
- A2: Trade bodies (FIVP, BVA, SPVS) offer an unpaid route to practice managers. Unverified.
