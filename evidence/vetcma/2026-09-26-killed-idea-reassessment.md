# Evidence: Vetcma post-kill reassessment

- **Idea / scope:** `vetcma` — broad CMA veterinary price-transparency/compliance toolkit
- **Register:** evidence + inference (append-only reassessment; original kill preserved)
- **Source:** CMA official case/guidance pages; Provet product page; prior fresh seed recheck
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-26
- **Credibility:** primary (CMA) plus vendor claim/testimonial (Provet)

## Claim(s) supported

- The CMA made and published its substantive Veterinary Services Market Investigation Order on 2026-09-22; the compliance duties and phased dates are real, but not all practices have the same deadline. [S1, S2]
- Written estimates apply to treatment pathways reasonably likely to cost at least £500 including VAT; estimates must be updated when expected total cost rises by 20% or £500, whichever is lower. Larger practices generally implement estimate/itemised-bill remedies by June 2027 and smaller practices by September 2027. [S2]
- Provet currently markets a PIMS workflow in which estimates are stored with health records and estimates flow to invoices/payment; its page includes a UK-practice testimonial about preparing/emailing estimates after the CMA ruling. [S3]
- A related fresh seed review found adjacent Provet, ezyVet and Vetstoria offers, but did not evidence a separate practice-side buyer or unmet bridge. [S4]

## Exact detail

- [S1] CMA case page: https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/veterinary-services-market-for-pets-review — final Order and RCVS undertakings published/made 2026-09-22.
- [S2] CMA business guidance, updated 2026-09-22: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-veterinary-businesses-and-vets-need-to-do-following-the-cmas-final-vets-report — describes price lists, estimates, revision threshold, itemised bills, phased deadlines and possible enforcement.
- [S3] Provet: https://www.provet.cloud/ — vendor page describes estimates in patient health records and estimate-to-invoice/payment workflow. The testimonial is vendor-selected.
- [S4] `runs/20260926T180413Z-normal/summary.md` and `evidence/vetcma/2026-09-21-cma-order-and-incumbent-landscape.md` — fresh seed recheck and the previously recorded CMA/competitor landscape. The non-inheriting `vet-estimate-bridge` seed was dropped as framed in that run.

## Why it is credible

The legal status and duties are from the CMA’s own pages. Provet’s features are directly described by the supplier and therefore establish an offered capability, not independent usage or satisfaction. The seed recheck is a dated research record, not demand evidence.

## What it does NOT show

- It does not show what proportion of practices have implemented compliant estimate and bill workflows, whether current PIMS products work well in practice, or whether owners pay separately for compliance software.
- Provet’s testimonial does not establish broad adoption, outcomes or a customer preference for Provet.
- It does not show a distinct legacy-PIMS segment, manual bridge spend, non-paid distribution or viable newcomer economics.
- A statutory workload and deadline do not by themselves establish a defensible software wedge.

## Reassessment (inference)

- **Buyer:** practice owners/managers are identifiable and the legal duty is firm; a separate software budget is not evidenced.
- **Problem:** the change materially increases the estimate/itemised-bill workload and corrects the former imprecise deadline description. It does not show that the workflow is unserved.
- **Alternatives / wedge:** the broad price-list toolkit remains occupied by the suppliers in the original evidence register; Provet now directly advertises the estimate-to-invoice/payment flow. `defensible_wedge` therefore remains `fail` for the idea as framed.
- **Distribution / economics:** trade-body access, paid conversion against embedded/free products, support costs and margins remain untested.
- **Risk / next evidence:** errors in legal thresholds or bills could harm practices and clients. Reopening would require evidence for a named practice/PIMS segment whose required flow is not supported by its current system, plus an already-paid workaround or other credible budget and a reachable route. No such evidence was found in this desk reassessment.
