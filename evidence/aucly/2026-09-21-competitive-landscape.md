## Claim(s) supported

- The UK school/PTA/charity auction category has multiple established providers with different pricing models:
  - **GalaBid** — free plan funded by optional checkout tips; Pro plan at 4.9%; strong for full galas with
    ticketing, tables and on-screen displays.
  - **Jumblebee** — long-established UK school fundraising platform; beeClassic charges 5% + VAT of winning
    bids; beeFree requires 10+ "Buzz" consignment items; beePro is fully managed on-site.
  - **PTA Events** — broader PTA platform (events, tickets, products, volunteers, communications); free
    Starter plan but roughly 5% on auction sales; the guide states it has processed over £40 million and
    3.4 million orders.
  - **Givergy (momoGood)** — fully managed, per-event pricing aimed at large galas.
  - **Generosity Works** — professionally run galas, £995/year standalone or free when booking founder Kevin
    Durham as auctioneer; live/sealed bids, pledges, Gift Aid, gala screen.
  - **Aucly** — flat fee per auction (see the pricing evidence register), no commission, self-service.
- Pricing across the category clusters into four patterns: percentage of winning bids, flat fee per
  auction/year, per-event managed pricing, and annual software.
- Aucly positions itself for self-service school/PTA auctions rather than staffed gala events.

## Exact detail

- BeeFree (Jumblebee's free tier) requires at least 10 consignment ("Buzz") items — i.e. a free option exists
  in the same self-service segment Aucly targets with its 15-item free tier.
- PTA Events' free Starter plan exists but takes ~5% of auction sales; the £40m/3.4m-orders claim is
  self-reported in Aucly's guide (attributed to PTA Events).
- GalaBid's free plan is funded by optional bidder checkout tips, so the organiser pays nothing.

## Why it is credible

The comparison is specific and dated, names real providers and prices, and was published publicly by the
Aucly team (who have reason to be accurate about competitors' public pricing). It is nonetheless a vendor
document; individual claims should be re-verified before being used for commitments.

## What it does NOT show

- It does not establish competitor revenue, customer counts, churn or market share.
- It does not demonstrate that Aucly's flat fee wins deals; no win/loss data is included.
- It cannot show whether the category as a whole is growing or shrinking.
- Free/low-cost tiers from GalaBid, Jumblebee and PTA Events mean Aucly's differentiation cannot be assumed
  from "we are cheaper".
