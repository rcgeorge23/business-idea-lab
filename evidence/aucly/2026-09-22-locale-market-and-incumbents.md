# Evidence: Non-UK locale market size and incumbent auction platforms (Ireland, US, Australia, New Zealand)

- **Idea / scope:** `aucly` (locale-expansion assessment)
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** Public statistical releases and incumbent vendor pricing pages, listed per
  claim below with publication/access dates.
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-22
- **Credibility:** mixed — primary for official statistics; primary (vendor's own
  pricing page) for incumbent pricing, which is a vendor claim about its own product.

## Claim(s) supported

**Market size (number of schools):**

- England has **24,499 schools** in 2025/26 (DfE, *Schools, pupils and their
  characteristics*, published 2026-07-14; 2024/25 was 24,479, published 2025-06-05).
- Ireland has **3,067 mainstream primary schools** (535,620 pupils, average 174.6)
  in 2025/26, plus **721 post-primary schools** — roughly **3,788 schools** total
  (Department of Education and Youth, *Preliminary Statistical Bulletin 2025/26*,
  census 30 September 2025, published February 2026).
- Australia has **9,673 schools** in 2025 (ABS, *Schools, 2025*, published
  2026-03-05).
- New Zealand has **2,536 schools** at 1 July 2025 (Education Counts, *Number of
  schools*).
- The United States has **99,297 operating public schools** in SY2023–24 across
  19,186 districts (NCES Common Core of Data, `202324_summary_2.asp`).

**Incumbent platforms and their pricing (the substitutes a new entrant must displace):**

- **New Zealand:** Toko (`toko.co.nz`, Auckland) advertises **100% free for NZ
  schools** and 5% for associations, with Windcave payments in NZD; it claims to be
  "the only website in the country offering this specialised service". Raised
  (`raised.nz`) is free for cash/bank transfer, with Stripe card fees paid by
  supporters.
- **Ireland:** Fundraising Solutions (`fundraisingsolutions.co`) advertises a
  **zero platform fee** and says it is "fully compliant with Irish fundraising
  guidelines"; iDonate.ie serves 5,000+ causes with online raffles at "lowest
  transaction fees"; FundRaisely.ie targets clubs/schools/PTAs with payment
  tracking/reconciliation; GalaBid publishes ROI-specific pricing.
- **Australia:** GalaBid is Sydney-based, runs ~1,000 events/year across Australia
  and New Zealand, prices region-specifically (free plan funded by optional tips,
  Pro 4.9%, flat US$1,250/campaign); AirAuctioneer and Oktion are named as cheaper
  options for small online-only auctions.
- **United States:** Auctria offers a **free Explorer tier** (up to US$10,000 event
  income/year, 250 bidders, 1% card fee), Emerald US$375/yr and Diamond US$750/yr,
  and states "no performance fees, ever" (auctria.com/pricing, accessed 2026-09-06).
  SchoolAuction.net (schools/PTAs/small nonprofits) starts at **US$79/event + 3%
  performance fee** (Acorn), rising to Essentials US$1,000/yr, Plus US$1,500/yr and
  Professional US$2,000/yr, and requires a US 501(c)(3) or business tax id
  (schoolauction.net/pricing, dated 2025-01-29).
- Global pricing patterns across these locales: free-with-optional-tips; % of
  winning bids; flat fee per event; annual subscription.

## Exact detail

- DfE release home for *Schools, pupils and their characteristics* states 24,499
  schools and ~8.9m pupils in 2025/26.
- Ireland *Preliminary Statistical Bulletin 2025/26*: 3,067 mainstream primary
  schools, 535,620 pupils, average 174.6 pupils per school, down 1.2% year on year;
  721 post-primary schools. CSO dataset ED106 was last updated 2026-09-21.
- ABS *Schools, 2025*: 9,673 schools (6,233 primary, 1,460 secondary, 1,441
  combined, 539 special; 69.6% government, 18.2% Catholic, 12.2% independent).
- Education Counts NZ: 2,536 schools at 1 July 2025 (1,927 primary, 197 composite,
  376 secondary, 36 specialist).
- NCES CCD: 99,297 operating public schools, SY2023–24, 19,186 districts.
- Toko's comparison page states "100% free for schools" and "5% for associations"
  and lists Windcave as the payment provider.
- Auctria pricing page (accessed 2026-09-06): Explorer free (event income cap
  US$10,000/yr, 250 bidders, 1% Auctria card fee); Emerald US$375/yr (US$50,000
  income, 750 bidders); Diamond US$750/yr (unlimited income, 1,000 bidders);
  optional paid Launch setup US$499–$749/event; US dollars for all customers
  except Canadians (CAD + HST); Stripe integrated; Auctria does not hold funds.
- SchoolAuction.net pricing (2025-01-29): Acorn US$79/event + 3% performance fee +
  3% + US$0.30 card; Essentials US$1,000/yr; Plus US$1,500/yr; Professional
  US$2,000/yr. Requires a valid 501(c)(3) or US business/non-profit tax id.

## Why it is credible

School counts come from official statistical agencies (DfE, Irish Department of
Education, ABS, NZ Education Counts, NCES). Incumbent pricing comes from the
vendors' own current pricing pages, which are reliable for *what is advertised*
though self-serving. GalaBid and Toko pricing are vendor claims and treated as
such.

## What it does NOT show

- It does **not** show willingness to pay, switching behaviour, or that any locale
  lacks an adequate tool. Free or low-cost incumbents already target exactly this
  segment in every locale examined, which is evidence **against** an easy wedge,
  not for one.
- School counts are not PTA/PTO counts; a school is not a guaranteed auction buyer
  and many schools run no auction at all.
- Vendor pricing pages do not prove take-up, revenue, retention, or that the free
  tiers are usable for a real event.
- It says nothing about Aucly's ability to reach any of these buyers unpaid.
