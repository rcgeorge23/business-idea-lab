## Claim(s) supported

- Real auctions have run on Aucly and at least three named organisations have used it: Furzedown Primary PTA,
  Lingfield Foundation (Lingfield College), and Chalford Hill.
- The Furzedown auction raised **£2,265** from **44 lots**, **63 unique bidders**, **1,074 bids** over a 7-day
  online auction (Spring 2026), with payment tracked by bank transfer and match funding.
- The Lingfield Foundation golf-day auction raised **£2,500** with bidding lasting approximately one month,
  promoted via social media and direct links; the organiser is quoted as finding Aucly easy to use and would
  recommend it to other schools or charities (publication pending organiser approval, feature-flag gated).
- Other live auction paths observed in analytics include `kimsfightforlife/champions-for-kim`,
  `cfdplanepull/cfd-plane-pull-dorset-mind`, `the-grand-fire-ride-2026` and
  `sltc/sltc-charity-day-tiebreak-auction`.

## Exact detail

- Furzedown case study (published 2026-06-18): "44 lots, 63 unique bidders, 1,074 bids, £2,265 raised" for
  playground refurbishment; top lots: Tooting Lido £185 (33 bids), Fortnum & Mason hamper £151 (77 bids),
  Exhibit Balham £128 (55 bids). The page describes self-service / free-tier usage.
- Lingfield case study (flag-gated, not yet published publicly): £2,500 raised alongside a golf day; audience =
  attendees plus the wider school community; cause = Foundation access/bursary work. Quote attributed to Sally
  (Lingfield Foundation/College): "We found Aucly very easy to use overall... I would definitely recommend Aucly
  to other schools or charities."
- Independent-schools landing page proof block: ~1 month of bidding, £2,500 raised, intent to use Aucly again.

## Why it is credible

These are Aucly's own published pages containing specific, checkable numbers (lots, bidders, bids, amounts) and
named organisations, with a live auction page for Chalford Hill appearing in search data. The numbers are
organiser-reported, not financial-statement evidence.

## What it does NOT show

- It does not show how many **paying** auctions these were (Furzedown documents free-tier/self-service usage).
- It does not establish repeat usage: "intent to use again" is stated but no second auction is recorded.
- It does not show acquisition channel cost, conversion rate from outreach, or retention.
- Case-study success numbers are curated; failed or abandoned auctions are not published.
