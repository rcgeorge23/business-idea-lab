# Evidence: PPWR application date and the existing compliance-software market

- **Idea / scope:** `packproof`
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR); environment.ec.europa.eu news (2026-08-11); business.gov.uk PPWR guidance; europen-packaging.eu (2026-07-08); sgs.com PPWR FAQ (2026-08-07); ppwrcopilot.com pricing and importers pages (2026-09-07 / 2026-07-04); ppwrify.de (2026-08-12); ppwrconnect.com; Trace One press release (2026-06-12; Paris, 2026-05-28); gov.uk "Extended producer responsibility for packaging" collection (updated 2026-08)
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21
- **Credibility:** EU regulation and government guidance are primary. Competitor pricing and capability claims are vendor self-descriptions — weak secondary only; they establish that positions are occupied and at what advertised price, not that those vendors are successful.

## Claim(s) supported

- PPWR creates real, dated new duties for packaged goods placed on the EU market, including for importers, with effect from 12 August 2026.
- Multiple credible providers already sell PPWR compliance software, including a UK-branded low-cost tier aimed at smaller businesses.
- The proposed wedge is therefore a feature of an established compliance-software category, already occupied at prices from £49/mo.

## Exact detail

- PPWR (Regulation (EU) 2025/40) entered into force 11 February 2025 and generally applies from 12 August 2026. PFAS limits in food-contact packaging apply from 12 August 2026 (Art 5(5)); traceability and labelling duties phase in from mid-2026 with harmonised EU labelling from 2028 (environment.ec.europa.eu; europen-packaging.eu, 2026-07-08; SGS FAQ, 2026-08-07).
- Importers/distributors must hold an EU Declaration of Conformity (single-use packaging 5 years; reusable 10 years) and make technical documentation available (PPWR Art 18(7), Art 39; PPWR Copilot importers page, last checked 2026-07-04). Around 30 delegated and implementing acts remain pending, with labelling implementing acts delayed to Q4 2026 — implementation detail is still unsettled.
- Trace One (press release 2026-06-12, event 2026-05-28) markets a three-pillar PPWR/EPR platform used by "9,000+ brands", citing PFAS thresholds (<25 ppb individual, <250 ppb sum, <50 ppm total fluorine) and an estimated 30,000 EU brand owners affected.
- PPWR Copilot (UK; PPWR Copilot Ltd, company no. 16636719, ICO ZB974172) advertises Starter £49/mo (10 dossiers), Pro £199/mo and Enterprise £499/mo across 27 EU countries plus the UK, with a 7-day free trial and a legal-advice disclaimer (ppwrcopilot.com, page dated 2026-09-07). PPWRify (Germany; ppwrify.de, 2026-08-12) advertises QR labelling (Art 12) and automatic EU DoC generation (Art 39). PPWR Connect (by ColorLoop) advertises DoC, recyclability grades, recycled content, EPR and Digital Product Passport.
- UK EPR for packaging is a separate domestic regime: large producers register annually, report every six months, buy PRNs/PERNs, pay a waste-disposal fee to PackUK and keep data seven years; small producers register and report by 1 April (gov.uk collection, updated 2026-08).

## Why it is credible

The regulatory dates and duties come from the EU institution's own pages and UK government guidance. The competitor facts are live, dated vendor pages that collectively show a populated market with low entry pricing.

## What it does NOT show

- Does not show how many small UK brands/importers are actually buying PPWR tooling, or their willingness to pay.
- Vendor pages are self-reported and unverified; no independent adoption or satisfaction data.
- Does not resolve whether the unsettled delegated acts create a temporary wedge or simply more incumbent churn.
