# Evidence: UK EPR small-producer obligations - shallow seed re-check

- **Idea / scope:** seed `uk-epr-small-producer-tooling` (origin idea `packproof`)
- **Register:** evidence
- **Source:** GOV.UK EPR guidance, Commons Library briefing CBP-10352, compliance-scheme pages
- **Accessed / dated:** 2026-09-21
- **Credibility:** primary (GOV.UK guidance) plus credible secondary (Commons Library, compliance schemes)

## Claim(s) supported

- A UK producer is obligated when it supplies/imports more than 25 tonnes of packaging in
  the previous calendar year and has a worldwide turnover of £1m or more.
- Small producers must register and report packaging data by 1 April each year; the 2025
  small-producer registration fee was £1,216, with a £332 late fee.
- A free GOV.UK "report packaging data" service exists, and established compliance
  schemes (Valpak, ERP, Countrystyle and others) serve the producer market.
- From the 2026-27 scheme year, fees move from flat base rates to a red/amber/green
  Recyclability Assessment Methodology modulation.
- Sources conflict on whether small producers must buy PRNs/PERNs: Countrystyle says
  they must; the Commons Library briefing (CBP-10352, 29 July 2026) says small producers
  are exempt from the waste disposal fee and from PRN/PERN obligations. This is recorded
  as unresolved.

## Why it is credible

Obligation thresholds, deadlines and fees come from primary GOV.UK guidance; the
Commons Library briefing is a credible secondary source. The PRN/PERN contradiction is
flagged rather than resolved.

## What it does NOT show

- It does not establish whether small producers feel any painful, budgeted burden, nor
  whether they would pay a third party beyond the free service and existing schemes.
- It does not resolve the PRN/PERN contradiction.
