## Seed hypothesis

MHCLG’s Awaab Phase 2 guidance states that expanded hazard response provisions are intended to apply from 2026-11-30 to England’s social rented sector. Emergency and significant hazards carry investigation, safety-work, tenant-communication and recordkeeping timelines. The guidance warns that awareness can reach a landlord through contractors or other agents, making intake timestamps, triage, escalation and evidence handoffs potentially consequential.

This is a lead about the workflow from first hazard awareness through investigation, tenant updates, safety action and closure. It is not yet an idea: no evidence in this run establishes that existing housing-management/repairs suites fail to support the required deadlines or that a social landlord would pay for a separate system.

## Why materially different from the origin idea

The origin idea concerns checking housing-repair invoices and Schedule-of-Rates lines before payment. This seed concerns hazard-notice intake, legal clocks, safety triage and communication with tenants across landlords and contractors. Shared sector lineage does not transfer any evidence, score or buyer assumptions.
