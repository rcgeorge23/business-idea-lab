# Seed: Written-estimate to itemised-bill bridge for independent vet practices

- **Seed ID / slug:** vet-estimate-bridge
- **Origin idea:** vetcma
- **Recorded:** 2026-09-21
- **Status:** unexplored

## Observation

The CMA's draft Order requires written estimates for treatment pathways
reasonably likely to cost GBP500 or more, and itemised bills for treatment
provided. Independent practices on legacy PMS or dated websites may lack a
fee-transparency workflow at the point of treatment, while the visible
incumbents market bundled compliance suites (price list plus complaints record)
rather than a bridge into the systems a practice already uses.

## Why this is materially different

It is a narrow workflow/integration wedge tied to fee capture during treatment,
not a price-list or compliance-suite product. It would live alongside an
existing PMS rather than replace it, and it targets a different sub-job than
the one the killed candidate addressed.
