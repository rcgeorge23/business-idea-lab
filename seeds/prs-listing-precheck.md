# Seed: PRS listing pre-check and landlord registration-number chase

- **Seed ID / slug:** prs-listing-precheck
- **Origin idea:** prsregister
- **Recorded:** 2026-09-21
- **Status:** unexplored

## Observation

Under the PRS Database regime, agents and advertisers are prohibited from
marketing a relevant rental property without an active Landlord Registration
Number and Property Registration Number. At the draft-regulations stage there
was no known bulk-upload or API route for agents, so the gap is per-property
checking and chasing landlords for current numbers.

## Why this is materially different

It is a validation/API wedge aimed at portals and CRM vendors (pre-marketing
checks and number freshness) rather than another agent-facing compliance suite.
It sells to the platforms that listings flow through, not to agents directly.
