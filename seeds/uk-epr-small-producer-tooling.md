## Observation

The PackProof kill found the EU PPWR compliance-software space crowded (PPWR Copilot,
PPWRify, PPWR Connect, Trace One, and others), so a broad EU packaging-compliance wedge
failed the defensible-wedge filter. During that review a separate regime was noted:

> **UK EPR for packaging is a distinct regime from EU PPWR.** Large UK producers register
> annually, report every six months, buy PRNs/PERNs and pay the PackUK waste disposal
> fee; small producers register and report by 1 April, with data retention duties. The
> EU-focused PPWR vendors may not serve this UK-only workflow.

## Why this is materially different from the parent

- **Different regulation and buyer:** UK EPR (PackUK, PRNs/PERNs) for UK producers, not
  EU PPWR labelling/DoC duties for exporters into the EU.
- **Different job:** recurring registration, reporting and PRN/PERN obligations, rather
  than packaging dossiers and Declarations of Conformity.
- **Different competitive set:** UK EPR compliance schemes and obligated-producer
  reporting tools, not EU PPWR dossier vendors.
- **Non-inheriting:** the parent's score (53.0), its kill reason and its `Plausible`
  evidence level are irrelevant to this seed; it starts at `unexplored` with no score.
