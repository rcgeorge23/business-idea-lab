## The corrected lens, stated precisely

The overturn exposed **two separate errors** that must not be conflated:

1. **"Currently done by a person" is not "occupied by a vendor."** A salaried employee performing a task is evidence the work is real and recurring — it is not evidence that a product would not be preferable. This error invalidated the O1 rejection.
2. **Absence of competitors is not evidence against an opportunity.** A seam with no incumbent is neutral-to-positive, not a reason to reject. The method already says competitor existence is not wedge failure; the converse (competitor absence is not wedge failure either) follows.

But there is a **third, legitimate** rejection ground that must not be weakened:

3. **Absent evidenced buyer is a real rejection ground.** A seam can be genuinely unoccupied and still fail because no buyer has been shown to have the problem, the budget, or a reachable route. This is a *buyer-evidence* failure, not a *competitor* failure, and it is legitimate.

The review below separates cases where error 1 or 2 applied (candidate for reconsideration) from cases where ground 3 applied (correctly rejected).

## Part 5 — Is there a systematic pattern?

The honest answer is **no, not in the kill record** — but **yes, in one specific triage habit**.

- **The kill record is sound.** All 15 kills rest on positive evidence. The corrected lens overturns none of them.
- **The triage habit is real but narrow.** The error was treating "the work is done by a person today" as equivalent to "the seam is occupied." That habit appeared once in six pools (O1). It is a genuine triage-depth finding, but it is not a systemic failure of the funnel.
- **The "absent evidenced buyer" ground is used heavily and correctly.** It accounts for most of the non-occupation rejections (O10, O16, O20, and the unoccupied-seam cases in the issue #15 run). This is the method working as designed: a seam can be unoccupied and still fail because nobody has shown they will pay.

## Recommendation

1. **No kill, seed or parked idea should be reopened** on the strength of the corrected lens alone. None of them failed on the invalid ground.
2. **Record the triage-depth finding for the next method review:** triage must not treat "currently performed by a person" as equivalent to "occupied by a vendor." The legitimate test is whether a credible alternative demonstrably occupies the exact seam. This is a clarification of existing rules, not a new rule, so it does not by itself require a method version bump — but it should be captured in `method/discovery.md`'s triage section at the next reviewable method change.
3. **Keep the "absent evidenced buyer" ground intact.** It is doing real work and the corrected lens must not be read as weakening it.
4. **The one live consequence is the new candidate** `housing-repairs-invoice-sor-validation`, which is parked at desk-screened (58.9/100) with the desk scan complete and the interview step awaiting owner approval.
