- Evidence type meanings: **primary** (official documents, first-party data),
  **practitioner** (forums, communities, support threads, job ads, service
  pricing), **vendor** (vendor marketing, release notes), **secondary**
  (journalism, analyst summaries).
- A rejected observation with an adjacent insight becomes a seed under `seeds/`,
  never a candidate.
- "I could not establish why this persists" is an acceptable archetype-B answer
  and should be recorded as triage evidence.
- O5 is a seed-sourced observation: the seed `vetlab-standard-conformance` was
  re-checked at the start of the run and then researched from scratch (its own
  evidence register, hard filters and scorecard); it inherits no score or
  evidence level from the killed parent `vetlab-bridge`.
- O10 was rejected on buyer economics and platform mediation, not on incumbent
  existence; the enterprise-availability-vs-niche-accessibility confusion was
  explicitly considered and did not change the outcome.
- Every rejection above records negative evidence and a reason, and no rejection
  rests on competitor existence alone.
