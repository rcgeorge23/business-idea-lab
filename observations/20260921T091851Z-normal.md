## Notes

- Evidence type meanings: **primary** (official documents, first-party data),
  **practitioner** (forums, communities, support threads, job ads, service
  pricing), **vendor** (vendor marketing, release notes), **secondary**
  (journalism, analyst summaries).
- A rejected observation with an adjacent insight becomes a seed under `seeds/`,
  never a candidate.
- "I could not establish why this persists" is an acceptable archetype-B answer
  and should be recorded as triage evidence.
- Triage here is shallow and does not replace the hard filters. Surviving triage
  confers no inherited positive evidence: `vetlab-bridge` and
  `clinicdata-liberation` were researched from scratch with their own evidence
  registers.
- Rejected observations O1, O19, O22, O23 and O28 were regulatory and are
  rejected principally for crowding/channel-ownership, not for being
  regulation-derived as such. The two promotions are both non-regulatory, so the
  source-class budget is met with room to spare.
- Both promoted observations were later killed after full research and an
  adversarial review: `vetlab-bridge` failed `defensible_wedge` (ManuCare,
  DataHub Vet) and `clinicdata-liberation` failed `defensible_wedge`
  (ValueStreamAI, free receiving-vendor migration). Neither promoted observation
  inherited any positive evidence into a surviving candidate. Two non-inheriting
  adjacent seeds were recorded: `seeds/vetlab-standard-conformance` and
  `seeds/clinic-legacy-managed-archive`.

## Triage false-negative audit (added 2026-09-21, issue #8, method 1.6.0)

The first sampled audit under method 1.6.0 re-checked this pool's strongest
high-ambiguity rejection. It is recorded here because the run's own output is a
frozen artefact; future funnel runs record the audit in the run summary and in the
pool file at run time.

- **Observation selected:** O14 (insurance broker submission re-keying).
- **Why selected:** relatively strong practitioner + vendor evidence among the
  rejections, and rejected precisely for incumbent occupation - the rejection class
  the 1.5.0 review asked to sample.
- **Original triage reasoning:** rejected because a live vendor (Ergini) appeared
  already to occupy the exact seam, beside Acturis/Applied Epic, Open GI/Acturis
  connectivity and PathwayPort.
- **Additional evidence checked (cheap, 3 searches, 2026-09-21):** Applied
  Systems/Ivans 2026 agency-carrier connectivity survey (74% of agents cite portal
  re-keying pain; 90% have reduced business over submission friction; commercial
  submission automation the #1 wanted capability); multiple live vendors with
  demonstrated deployments in the seam (CogniSure, Kalepa, Heron Data UK MGA case
  study, Unitary, Insillion InFlow, Argonaut, SubmitIQ, Broker Buddha/AgencyAssist,
  Agiliux); targeted re-verification of "Ergini" did not confirm it - recorded
  honestly, the wider vendor field was verified instead.
- **Five confusion tests:** existence vs satisfaction (no - deployments show real
  use); feature vs complete solution (complete intake-to-portal flows, not
  features); enterprise vs niche (UK MGA/broker deployments show mid-market access);
  vendor claims vs demonstrated capability (measured case studies, named customers);
  one-shot vs recurring (recurring need, but served).
- **Outcome: rejection upheld.** Occupation is evidenced, not assumed from
  competitor existence alone.
- **Implication for triage depth:** no change; the competitor-existence
  clarification did not force promotion here, and there is no repeated-overturn
  signal.
