### Persistent-market-failure check

For O4–O15, the sweep found descriptions of paid routines, service offerings or an
existing statutory workflow, but did not establish the two-part persistence thesis
needed to lift the missing-why-now cap for a candidate: (1) continued target-segment
pain/workaround despite an accessible alternative and (2) a cited, present-tense
mechanism explaining why that alternative has not resolved it. Where a job ad names
manual work, it does not explain whether incumbent tools are inadequate; where a
vendor prices a service, it does not establish buyer dissatisfaction or an unmet
software job. CIS has explicit free and commercial alternatives. No persistent
observation was promoted, and no persistence thesis was treated as proven.
