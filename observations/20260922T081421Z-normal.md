| Confusion tests | Existence vs satisfaction: the incumbent is not merely present, it implements the full workflow and is priced at GBP 9.99/month. Feature vs solution: the incumbent is a complete solution for capture, acknowledgement and invoicing, not a partial feature. Enterprise vs niche: TradePlanr has a free plan and a GBP 9.99/month tier aimed at micro-firms, so it is accessible to the target niche. Claims vs capability: the feature page describes shipped functionality, not a roadmap. One-shot vs recurring: variations recur on every job, and the incumbent charges monthly, so the economics are recurring. |
| Outcome | Upheld |
| Implication for triage depth | Triage depth was adequate for this observation. The rejection rests on a complete, cheap, accessible incumbent rather than on competitor existence alone. One caveat recorded: the satisfaction evidence is the incumbent's own feature page, so the audit cannot rule out that micro-firms find the workflow too heavy - but no practitioner evidence of that was found, and the burden of proof sits with the observation, not the incumbent. |
