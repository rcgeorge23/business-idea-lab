An observation is not an idea: it records an evidenced problem, workflow,
dissatisfaction, market failure or change without a product proposition. Rows are
not scored, carry no evidence level, and confer no inherited positive evidence on
any candidate later promoted from them (see `method/discovery.md`).

**Frame for this sweep (deliberate change of frame, per the previous run's
guidance).** This is an owner-directed **locale-expansion frame**, not a general
opportunity sweep: every observation bears on whether a UK-run school/charity
auction platform (Aucly) could or should enter Ireland, the US, Australia or New
Zealand. That frame was chosen because the previous run explicitly asked for a
different-shaped sweep (buyers who already pay a provider; thinner incumbent
sets). It produces observations, not candidates; the assessment that consumes
them is `ideas/aucly/expansion-assessment.md`.

## Triage false-negative audit

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Observation selected | **O20** — US schools/PTAs demonstrably pay for this category (Auctria US$375–750/yr; SchoolAuction.net US$1,000–2,000/yr) |
| Why selected | It is the most promising/high-ambiguity rejection in the pool: it is the only observation showing **money already moving** in an expansion locale, so a rejection based on "crowded" risked the existence-vs-satisfaction confusion test. High ambiguity warranted a second look. |
| Original triage reasoning | Rejected because the budget is occupied at both ends — a free tier (Auctria Explorer, ≤US$10k event income) underneath and $79/event–$2,000/yr plans above — with no evidenced organiser dissatisfaction. |
| Additional evidence checked | Auctria pricing/plan structure (accessed 2026-09-06); SchoolAuction.net pricing and 501(c)(3)/tax-id requirement (2025-01-29); GalaBid US pricing (free-with-tips / 4.9% / US$1,250); the Charleston-Principles solicitation context (IRS, reviewed 2026-06-28). Specifically checked whether Auctria's free tier leaves an unserved small-PTA seam and whether any incumbent shows *unsatisfied* users. |
| Outcome | **Upheld.** The free tier covers the small end (so "cheap self-service" is not an available wedge), the paid tiers prove budget but also prove incumbency, and no source showed organiser dissatisfaction or migration. Rejection stands, with the reason restated as "satisfied at both price ends", not merely "competitors exist". |
| Implication for triage depth | Adequate for a cost/benefit assessment. It does expose one unexamined seam worth a future look: Auctria's free tier **caps event income at US$10,000**, so a US probe aimed at slightly larger PTAs would need to test willingness to pay above the free-tier ceiling rather than below it. |

## Notes

- Evidence type meanings: **primary** (official documents, first-party data),
  **practitioner** (forums, communities, support threads, job ads, service
  pricing), **vendor** (vendor marketing, release notes), **secondary**
  (journalism, analyst summaries).
- A rejected observation with an adjacent insight becomes a seed under `seeds/`,
  never a candidate. No seed was warranted here: the observations are expansion
  cost/market facts about an existing idea, not adjacent propositions that
  survive on their own.
- "I could not establish why this persists" is an acceptable archetype-B answer
  and should be recorded as triage evidence.
- **Money-already-moving outcome (v1.8.0):** 4 observations (O2, O3, O4, O20) came
  from money-already-moving sources (service/agency pricing and incumbent pricing).
  They yielded **budgeted category spend but no available wedge** — in every locale
  the spend is already captured by a free or low-cost incumbent. No unsuccessful
  search is hidden: the searches that returned nothing usable were for IE/NZ/AU
  practitioner complaints about incumbents (no relevant forum threads surfaced),
  and for any locale-specific evidence of organisers switching platforms (none
  found).

  figure, which the owner has since stated is largely bot traffic (Cloudflare's
  bot-filtered count was 130 visits, an ~18x gap to the application counter). O1's

  `evidence/aucly/2026-09-22-acquisition-bot-correction.md`. The table above is
