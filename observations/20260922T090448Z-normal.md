## Notes

- Evidence type meanings: **primary** (official documents, first-party data),
  **practitioner** (forums, communities, support threads, job ads, service
  pricing), **vendor** (vendor marketing, release notes), **secondary**
  (journalism, analyst summaries).
- A rejected observation with an adjacent insight becomes a seed under `seeds/`,
  never a candidate.
- "I could not establish why this persists" is an acceptable archetype-B answer
  and should be recorded as triage evidence.
- A latent-opportunity observation (archetype C) is admissible only with the six
  required fields above; "customers do not know they need it yet" is never a
  substitute for a buyer, mechanism, distribution route or falsifiable test.
- **Seed candidates from this run (not yet written to `seeds/`):** O1 and O15 both
  point at the same underlying seam from opposite sides — provider-side
  invoice-to-SOR validation (O1) and contractor-side SOR-to-invoice reconciliation
  (O15). The audit found the contractor side already served by Assignar Pay and the
  provider side already marketed as AP reconciliation assistance by Ingentive, so
  neither was promoted. If a seed is recorded it should be a single
  `housing-repairs-invoice-sor-validation` seed capturing the provider-side
  question, with the explicit note that the contractor side is occupied.
- **Money-already-moving outcome (v1.8.0):** the frame worked as intended — it
  produced budget evidence that complaint forums do not carry (salaried
  reconciliation roles at GBP 30,000–38,000, published outsourced-bookkeeping
  rates, a council audit finding of an invoice-authorisation backlog, quantified

  every seam with proven spend was either occupied by established vendors or

  finding: budget evidence is necessary but not sufficient, and the binding
  constraint remains that a widely-budgeted seam is visible to incumbents.
