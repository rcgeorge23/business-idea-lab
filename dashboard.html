Why now?

What changedA 2026 end-of-life set forces migrations: BP Allied switches off on 31 July 2026 with no further support, Cherwell Service Management reaches end-of-life on 31 December 2026, and on-premise RoboVet reached end-of-life with a UK practice migrating in June 2026.
When2026-07-31
Why it mattersA fixed switch-off forces an action clinics would otherwise defer, and uncertified niche platforms carry no legal export obligation, so extraction has to be done by someone.
Strengthstrong
Competitors respondedYes - ValueStreamAI already sells UI-level extraction of no-export clinical systems at published bands and receiving vendors (Nookal, Upheal) ship guided migration and give it away in onboarding.
